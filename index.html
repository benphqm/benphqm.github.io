Like I say, March is always a good month. I was losing it in February because of how stuck I felt. Life felt sort of meaningless and there were days that I was unhappy. January and February are always months that hold on to the previous year. People celebrate the new year but they haven't internalized that it's another year yet. They carry themselves the same way. And it isn't until we learn to stop erasing the prior year and writing the correct one when we're writing dates does the new year start.

March has always been a nice green color. It was the green of my A &f zip hoodie, it was the green of the cactopus and Princess Mononoke, Mt. Tam and playing tennis and driving thru Orinda on the way to piano class. I spent a lot of money over break and I have been spending a lot recently. I went all over the place in break, driving hundreds of miles in an Acura with 202,000 miles on its odometer, that probably shouldn't even be leaving Dublin. I jumped over fences to climb up hills, bought overpriced milk tea, saw the ocean. I just went to see Kanye West at Sofi stadium in LA on the first wednesday of the quarter. It was around 120 dollars, and while the Friday show turned way up (They had Lauryn Hill, Ceelo, AND Travis Scott?) I was happy I got to see Kanye. I really hate people who idolize a figure (we see how that went with Ye), and it's so strange that thousands of people pay so much just to be in proximity of some other human. But it was interesting when I looked at his figure on the stage, barely visible from the seats I paid for that this was the man who made all the music that sort of defined these large parts of my life, like the evenings after school at wednesday band practice and the countless bike rides. But it's that way for so many people I guess. That's why people say he's a legend. It's just from the impact someone who will never know you has on your life.

Not every week can be fun. I mean I guess I might go to san diego again on the second weekend. well see. But I have a lot to think about as my first year wraps up here. Doing more job apps, but have I made peace with not having a job the same way I made peace with not getting into the BS clubs here? possibly, and I have some leeway as I'm staying an extra summer. But I also think I'm getting a little complacent. It feels that way that I'll never be good enough for applications given my status as a junior compared to my peers, and so I don't even bother applying. The least qualified person in the world would have a higher chance of being selected for a position than me if I don't apply though, no?
